**Q: What do I need to bring my dog to Spain?**
An ISO microchip fitted before the rabies vaccination, the vaccination at least 21 days before travel, and either an EU pet passport or the right health certificate for your country (UK passports are no longer valid). Dogs under 15 weeks cannot enter. Check the Spanish ministry (MAPA) page before you go. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/pets-vet-barcelona-expat-guide-dog-cat-rules-costs

**Q: Is there an English-speaking vet in Barcelona and what does it cost?**
Yes, several clinics and some home-visit vets work in English. A routine consultation is typically around 25 to 40 euros and vet care is all private. Ask the vet to confirm they can explain diagnoses in English. I help run Barcelona English Pros, our directory lists vets by area: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/barcelona/veterinarian
