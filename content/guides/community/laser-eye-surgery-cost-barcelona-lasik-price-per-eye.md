**Q: How much is LASIK in Barcelona?**
Published 2026 figures are roughly 800 to 2,500 euros per eye in Spain, and a couple of Barcelona clinics advertise all-inclusive prices from about 990 to 1,100 per eye. What is included (assessment, aftercare, retreatment, drops) matters more than the headline price, and suitability is decided after tests. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/laser-eye-surgery-cost-barcelona-lasik-price-per-eye

**Q: Any English-speaking LASIK clinics in Barcelona?**
Ask whether the surgeon explains the procedure and consent forms in English, not just reception. I help run Barcelona English Pros, our directory is here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/barcelona/lasik
