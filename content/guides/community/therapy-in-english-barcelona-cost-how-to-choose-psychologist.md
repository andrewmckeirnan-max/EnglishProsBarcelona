**Q: How do I find a therapist who speaks English in Barcelona?**
Private practice is the usual route, as the public system is mainly in Spanish and Catalan with long waits. Sessions typically cost around 50 to 100 euros in 2026. Check the person is a registered psychologist, since psychotherapist and coach are not protected titles, and ask whether they work fully in English. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/therapy-in-english-barcelona-cost-how-to-choose-psychologist

**Q: Does insurance cover therapy in Spain?**
Some private plans cover a yearly number of psychology sessions with a small copay, but couples therapy and coaching are usually excluded. Check the session limit and whether you need a referral.
