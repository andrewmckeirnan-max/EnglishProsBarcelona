**Q: How much does a wedding planner cost in Barcelona?**
Published 2026 ranges are roughly 2,000 to 5,000 euros for full planning in Barcelona, 600 to 1,800 for day-of coordination and 1,200 to 3,500 for partial planning. Some charge 10 to 15% of the budget instead of a flat fee. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/wedding-planner-cost-barcelona-spain-2026-fees

**Q: Do I need a planner for a destination wedding in Spain?**
Many international couples find one saves time and helps with vendor negotiation and legal paperwork, but it depends on budget and how involved you want to be. I help run Barcelona English Pros, our directory is here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/barcelona/wedding-planner
