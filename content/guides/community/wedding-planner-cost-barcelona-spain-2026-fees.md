**Q: How much does a wedding planner cost in Barcelona?**
For English-speaking destination planners, full planning is typically about 3,500 to 8,000 euros in 2026, often plus 21% VAT, partial planning around 2,000 to 4,000 and day-of coordination around 1,000 to 2,500. That is the planner's fee only, and a Barcelona wedding usually costs 35,000 or more in total.
