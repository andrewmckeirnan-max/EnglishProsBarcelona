**Q: How much does a gestor cost in Spain?**
For a freelancer in 2026 the monthly fee is roughly 25 to 60 euros for a basic online service and 60 to 120 for a face-to-face office, before VAT. Extras such as the annual tax return (30 to 150), registration and replies to tax office notices are usually charged on top, so ask for the full price list. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/gestor-cost-spain-autonomo-tax-return-registration-prices

**Q: Is a gestor worth it as an autónomo?**
Usually yes in the first year, since a late or wrong filing costs more than the fee. Check what forms and how many invoices the price covers and whether they work in English.
