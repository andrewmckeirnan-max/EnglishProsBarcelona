**Q: How much extra do I pay when buying a flat in Barcelona?**
Guides for 2026 suggest budgeting roughly 8 to 13% on top of the price. The biggest item is the transfer tax on resale homes, about 10% in Catalonia (new builds pay VAT and stamp duty instead), then notary (about 600 to 1,200), registry, legal help and a valuation if you borrow. Check with a lawyer for your case. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/buying-property-barcelona-costs-taxes-notary-total

**Q: Who pays the estate agent?**
In a sale it is normally the seller, typically 3 to 5% plus VAT in Barcelona. For rentals the landlord pays since the 2023 housing law. I help run Barcelona English Pros: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/renting-in-barcelona-expat-guide-deposit-contract-rent
