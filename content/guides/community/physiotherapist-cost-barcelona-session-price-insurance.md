**Q: How much does a physio cost in Barcelona?**
Published clinic prices in 2026 are mostly around 60 to 80 euros for a 50 to 60 minute private session, with a wider range of about 35 to 120. Packs of 5 or 10 bring the per-session price down, and some private insurance reimburses up to 80% if it has a free-choice option. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/physiotherapist-cost-barcelona-session-price-insurance

**Q: Any English-speaking physios in Barcelona?**
Several clinics treat fully in English. Confirm the therapist, not just reception, speaks it. I help run Barcelona English Pros, our directory lists them by area: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/barcelona/physiotherapist
