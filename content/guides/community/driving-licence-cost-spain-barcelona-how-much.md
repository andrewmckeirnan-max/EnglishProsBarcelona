**Q: How much does it cost to get a Spanish driving licence?**
In Barcelona in 2026 expect roughly 800 to 1,500 euros all in: school registration, theory, a fixed DGT exam fee of about 94 euros, a medical certificate (30 to 60) and 20 to 40 lessons at 29 to 45 each. Low package prices usually leave out the lessons. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/driving-licence-cost-spain-barcelona-how-much

**Q: Can I just exchange my foreign licence?**
Often yes if your country has an agreement, and the DGT fee is 28.87 euros for a car licence without exams. I help run Barcelona English Pros, our exchange guide is here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/exchange-driving-licence-spain-expat-guide
