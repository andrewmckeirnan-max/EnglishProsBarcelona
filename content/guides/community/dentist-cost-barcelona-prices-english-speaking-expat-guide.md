**Q: How much should I expect to pay at a dentist in Barcelona?**
Dentistry here is mostly private, so prices vary by clinic. Published ranges for 2026 are roughly 50 to 150 euros for a filling, 300 to 800 for a crown and 800 to 1,800 for a single implant. Ask for a written, itemised quote before agreeing to anything big, and get a second one for major work. I help run Barcelona English Pros, there is a longer guide with a quote checklist here: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/blog/dentist-cost-barcelona-prices-english-speaking-expat-guide

**Q: Any English-speaking dentists in Barcelona?**
Plenty of clinics work in English, but check that the dentist, not just reception, speaks it, especially for treatment plans and consent forms. Look for clinics with an English site and confirm by phone. I help run Barcelona English Pros, and our directory lists dentists by area: https://www.barcelonaenglishpros.com/barcelona/dentist
