## What to offer

Do not just ask for a link. Give them something their readers can use:

- **The free cost checker** (`/cost-checker`): sourced 2026 price ranges for about 30 services, useful to any relocation blog.
- **The price guides** (dentist, therapy, physio, gestor, driving licence, buying costs, LASIK, wedding planner).
- **Correct facts.** If their page has an out-of-date price or a business that has closed, send the correction with a source.
- **A directory listing** for the resource pages that list English-speaking professionals.
- **Data on request.** If they are writing about costs, offer a comment or the underlying ranges with sources.

Be honest about who you are: you run the site, it is free for visitors, and you may earn a fee from professionals.
